# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the LinWin project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, giving and receiving constructive feedback, respecting differing viewpoints, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, insults, discriminatory language, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository hosting provider. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
